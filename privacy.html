Privacy Policy

How Cash Deliver intends to collect, use, and protect information when customers browse products or request local delivery.

Important: This is a starter policy template for your website prototype, not a legal review. Before accepting real orders, replace the placeholders, confirm your actual data practices, and get appropriate legal advice for your business.

1. About this policy

Cash Deliver is a local shopping website being developed to serve selected areas of Pilkhuwa and Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, India. This policy explains how personal information may be handled when the service becomes operational.

2. Information we may collect

When the website is connected to an order system, information may include:

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your delivery address, locality, and delivery instructions.
  • Products requested, quantities, order reference, and order status.
  • Messages you send to customer support.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

Only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose. Do not request passwords, payment card details, or unrelated sensitive information through ordinary order forms.

3. How information may be used

Subject to the actual service and applicable law, information may be used to:

  • Confirm and deliver customer orders.
  • Contact customers about delivery availability or order changes.
  • Respond to customer questions, complaints, or replacement requests.
  • Maintain order records and help prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and accounting obligations.

4. Cash on Delivery

Cash Deliver currently plans to offer Cash on Delivery only. The prototype does not process online payments. If other payment methods are introduced later, this policy should be updated to describe the payment provider and the information it handles.

5. Sharing information

Customer information should only be shared when necessary to operate the service, such as with a delivery helper or technology provider, or when required by law. Before launch, identify any actual providers that may process customer information and explain their roles clearly. Do not sell personal information.

6. Data storage and security

The current HTML prototype does not securely store or submit customer orders to a business database. A live service should use suitable security controls, restrict access to customer details, protect administrator accounts, and keep information only as long as needed for legitimate business or legal purposes.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The website may use browser storage for features such as a shopping cart. Browser storage is not the same as a secure order database and should not be used to store sensitive customer information. Any additional analytics or tracking tools should be disclosed before use.

8. Information retention

A live business should establish a reasonable retention period based on fulfilment, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution, and applicable legal requirements. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

9. Your choices and requests

Customers should have a clear way to ask about their information, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law. Add a verified contact method before publishing this policy.

10. Children's privacy

The service is intended for ordinary household shopping. A live service should not knowingly collect children's personal information except as permitted by applicable law and with any required safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may need to change when the website adds an order database, messaging integration, analytics, new payment methods, or delivery partners. Publish the revised policy and its effective date whenever material changes are made.

12. Contact for privacy questions

Before launch, add your business's verified support email or postal contact details here. Do not publish personal contact information unless you intend to use it for customer support.
